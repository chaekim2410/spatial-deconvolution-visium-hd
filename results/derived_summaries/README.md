# Derived summaries

These small CSV files were calculated from retained standard-Visium matrices or group-project HD outputs. They are included because they support the final claims while avoiding redistribution of raw or spot-level HD data.

- `rctd_all_parts_metrics.csv` and `cell2location_part0_metrics.csv`: numeric full-versus-split checks.
- `visium_hd_part_composition.csv`: average cell-type composition by method and HD partition.
- `visium_hd_spot_usage.csv`: method-specific used and rejected HD spots by partition.
